About

Therapy and Training Solutions Ltd is a provider of Psychological Therapies and Mental Health Wellness training for businesses and their employees.

We also provide ancillary services which includes 1:1 therapist supervision and general consultation.

Cognitive and Behavioural Psychotherapy is the main treatment modality. Our pool of therapists are all BABCP accredited clinicians and will have completed a university accredited BSc Hons/PG Dip/MSc course in Cognitive and Behavioural Psychotherapy. NICE Guidelines for the treatment of all Clinical Anxiety Disorders and Clinical Depression, are adhered to in line with best and evidence-based practice.
